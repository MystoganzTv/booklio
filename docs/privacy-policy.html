Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026  ·  Effective: May 28, 2026

Bookliz is a free personal reading tracker supported by advertising. We collect only what is necessary to save your library, sync it across your devices, and serve relevant ads. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

Bookliz is developed and operated by Mysto Dev ("we", "us", or "our"). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at enrique.padron853@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

When you use Bookliz, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account identifiers — email address and display name, provided during sign-up or through Apple Sign-In / Google Sign-In.
  • User ID — a randomly generated UUID that links your data to your account.
  • Reading content you create — books you add to your library, reading sessions (pages read, time, notes), reviews, favorite quotes, series tracking data, and personal rankings.
  • App preferences — language choice (English or Spanish), theme (light or dark), and annual reading goal. These are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your account.

We do not collect location data, payment information, health data, contacts, or photos.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To store your library and sync it across devices where you sign in with the same account.
  • To calculate your reading statistics, streaks, and achievements within the app.
  • To restore your data if you reinstall the app or switch devices.
  • To show you advertisements that help keep Bookliz free.

4. Advertising

Bookliz displays ads provided by third-party advertising networks. These networks may use cookies, device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID / IDFA on iOS), and similar technologies to serve ads that are relevant to your interests, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud.

On iOS, when you first open the app you will see Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt asking for permission to use your Advertising Identifier (IDFA). If you allow tracking, our ad partner may use the IDFA to show personalized ads and measure campaign performance. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

You can change your ATT preference at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

The data collected by our advertising partner for ad delivery is governed by their own privacy policy — see the Third-party services section below.

5. Third-party services

Bookliz relies on the following third-party services:

  • Supabase (privacy policy) — our backend database and authentication provider. Your email, user ID, and reading data are stored on Supabase servers hosted in the EU (AWS eu-central-1) by default.
  • Apple Sign-In (privacy policy) — optional sign-in method. Apple may share a relay email address instead of your real email.
  • Google Sign-In (privacy policy) — optional sign-in method. Google provides your name and email to complete account creation.
  • Advertising network — [insert network name and privacy policy link here before publishing] — serves ads within the app and may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad targeting and measurement.
  • Open Library (Internet Archive) (terms) — when you tap "Fetch missing info", the book title or ISBN is sent to Open Library's public API to retrieve metadata (cover image, page count, synopsis). No personal data is sent.

6. Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app. This permanently erases your library, reading sessions, achievements, and profile from our servers. The deletion is irreversible.

7. Data security

All data in transit between the app and Supabase is encrypted with TLS. Supabase enforces row-level security so that each user can only access their own data. Sensitive credentials (auth tokens) are stored in the iOS Secure Enclave via expo-secure-store, not in plain UserDefaults.

8. Children's privacy

Bookliz is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at enrique.padron853@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify you via an in-app message on your next launch.

11. Contact
